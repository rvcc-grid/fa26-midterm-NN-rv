Nikos's Guide for Building a Webstie

This website gives you advice on how to build your own website in three parts right below.

Art of Coding

Part 1: Building the Foundation

Building the foundation of your site before anything else is the first thing to do as you need to set up everything from the header to the body and then to the footer on what you want on your site.

Part 2: Painting with Colors

Once the site foundation is settled you can start to add colors of your choice and it's recomended to stick to a color pallet to have good blends of your colors on the site you created.

Part 3: Coding any last minute tweeks or changes

Adjust any minor code if needed in order to make sure your site fully works and functions well and once your happy with your code you would have fully created your website.
